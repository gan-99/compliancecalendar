About & method

ComplianceCalendar.au tracks Australian business compliance deadlines, regulator changes and legislation in progress — and publishes them in plain English, free.

How we source entries

  • Primary sources only: the Federal Register of Legislation, Parliament of Australia, and regulator publications (ATO, ASIC, OAIC, ACCC, FWO, Home Affairs, state revenue offices, state WHS and health regulators).
  • Every entry carries a link to its primary source so you can check it yourself.
  • Entries marked verify are published with an explicit caveat — we have not yet confirmed the date or scope against the primary source.

What we do and don't do

We describe obligations, dates and penalties. We do not give legal, tax or accounting advice, and nothing here should be relied on as advice for your specific circumstances. If an obligation applies to you, confirm it with your accountant, lawyer or the regulator.

Corrections

If something here is wrong, tell us and we will correct it publicly and promptly. Accuracy is the entire product.
